Friday, XVII Week of Ordinary Time

Lectio Divina

Matthew 13:54-58

1

Lectio (Read)

Jesus came to his native place and taught the people in their synagogue in such a way that they were astonished and said, "Where did this man get such wisdom and mighty deeds?"

And they took offense at him. But Jesus said to them, "A prophet is not without honor except in his native place and in his own house." And he did not work many mighty deeds there because of their lack of faith.

2

Meditatio (Reflect)

Take a few moments of quiet reflection. Consider these questions:

  • What word or phrase resonates most in my heart today?
  • Where in my daily life am I showing a lack of faith in God's presence?
3

Oratio (Pray)

"Lord, increase my faith. Help me to recognize Your wisdom in the ordinary moments and people of my life."

4

Contemplatio & Actio (Rest & Act)

Rest silently in His presence for a moment, then take this resolution into your day:

"Today I will pray for someone in my family or workplace whom I usually find difficult to accept."
